HOW TO PLAY

Survive the endless night. Zombies pour at the fortress gate. Kill them, pick up what they drop by walking over it, and spend it on weapons, facilities and Mechanic Pets. You fall — or the Keep falls — and the run ends. Everything you build is permanent.

Controls: WASD move · mouse aim · click/hold fire · R reload · Space dash · Shift sprint · E use a building · 1–9 switch weapon · G grenade · H medkit · T tower Target Attack (then click a zombie; usable once every two waves) · Esc pause. On touch screens: twin sticks + buttons.

The fortress: the Workshop builds pets and produces ammo, grenades and mines. The Medical Station heals and crafts medkits. The Warehouse caps how much you can store. The Keep opens Fortress Upgrades — six permanent facility lines. Guard towers auto-fire at random targets; walk to one to upgrade it. Build pads take turrets, MG nests, flame traps and spike strips.

Mechanic Pets: build up to as many as you like — 2 active at once. A pet deals 20% of your damage and each hit can proc a buff or debuff (10%). Each pet carries at most 2 skills, installed and combined at the Workshop with Pet Skill Stones (dropped by elites and bosses): Auto Pickup fetches drops for you, HP Regeneration heals you every second and stacks, One Hit Kill rolls 10% on normal zombies, 1% on elites and 0.01% on a boss below 75% HP.

The horde: Walkers and Runners first, then Crawlers (they slip through wall gaps), Tanks that smash structures, Exploders, Screamers that call more, Armored with a weak back, and Mutants. Every 10th wave a boss comes — Brute, Butcher, Abomination, Commander, Colossus — each with telegraphed special attacks. Watch the red rings and lines: move.
